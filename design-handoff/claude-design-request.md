# Claude Design request

Design Chairpedia's signature Chair Fit Calculator using the attached interaction brief and asset manifest.

This must be a high-fidelity, production-oriented interactive product design rather than a generic survey redesign. The defining experience is a living Body–Chair–Desk fitting model that responds to user inputs, produces evidence-backed chair matches, and transitions naturally into real showroom discovery.

Please inspect these live references before designing:

- https://www.chairpedia.com/
- https://www.chairpedia.com/chair
- https://www.chairpedia.com/find-your-chair
- https://www.chairpedia.com/stores
- https://www.chairpedia.com/products
- https://www.chairpedia.com/compare

Use the current Chairpedia brand as a continuity reference, while giving the calculator a more distinctive and premium art direction.

The public experience must be English only.

## First response

Before producing final screens, return:

1. Three distinct creative concepts
2. The signature interaction for each concept
3. The proposed desktop and mobile structure
4. Performance and implementation risks
5. A recommendation of which concept should proceed

The concepts must differ meaningfully in layout and interaction, not merely in color.

## Selected-concept output

After a concept is selected, produce:

1. Desktop opening, all input states, analysis transition, results, comparison, and showroom flow
2. Mobile equivalents for every critical state
3. Responsive component system
4. Code-ready Body–Chair–Desk vector layers
5. Fit-range visualization
6. Fit Passport and privacy-safe share templates
7. Good Fit, Conditional Fit, and Insufficient Data states
8. Confirmed-to-try and Brand-carried showroom states
9. Empty, loading, unavailable, denied, and error states
10. Reduced-motion specification
11. Clickable prototype from start through showroom selection
12. Developer handoff with exact dimensions, breakpoints, tokens, component variants, and animation timing

## Quality requirement

The result should be bold, animated, refined, and highly shareable, but it must remain fast, factual, accessible, and usable on an ordinary mobile device.

Do not use long intro animations, scroll-jacking, heavy WebGL, fake product photos, decorative maps, vague AI claims, or unexplainable recommendation percentages.

The final experience must make it immediately clear:

- what physically fits,
- what may not fit,
- what information is missing,
- where the chair can be tried,
- and what the user should do next.
