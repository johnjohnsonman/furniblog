# ChairPark Character Asset Archive

> **Scope:** ChairMan and ChairBot are ChairPark character IP. They must not appear in the Chairpedia calculator, catalog, editorial pages, evidence tables, or showroom finder. The active Chairpedia direction is `chairpedia-authority-upgrade-brief.md`.

This file preserves the approved character identity constraints for future ChairPark work. It is not an active Chairpedia design brief.

## ChairMan canonical identity

- Use the original ChairMan renders supplied before the trouser experiments as the identity reference.
- Keep the large softly asymmetrical head, broad cheeks, larger vertical oval eyes, small curved eyebrows, compact smile, wide padded torso, rounded belly, oversized hands, thick segmented limbs, and low center of gravity.
- Use the original soft charcoal vest with broad collar and hanging rectangular character badge.
- Add soft matte charcoal trousers as a lower-body wardrobe edit. Do not reinterpret the face, head, hands, torso, vest, badge, material, or overall proportions while adding them.
- Keep compact white feet with dark soles visible below the trousers.
- In seated poses, both complete soles must touch the floor and the knees should remain approximately 90–100 degrees.
- Lower the chair only to a mechanically believable setting. If the chair is already at its limit, modestly lengthen both lower-leg segments while preserving their plush diameter and the character’s short, stocky silhouette.
- Avoid a generic puffer jacket, zipper, pockets, round chest pin, small close-set eyes, missing eyebrows, spherical mannequin head, clenched fists, or a tall and slim body.

## ChairBot canonical identity

- Compact white robot with a dark face display and mineral-blue signals.
- Preserve the compact chair-like base, small proportions, and simple functional expressions.
- Approved face states: neutral, measuring, confirmed, caution, and unknown data.
- Expressions communicate status. ChairBot should not become a chat interface.

## Production asset rules

- Approve one master image for each character before creating additional poses.
- Generate every pose as a separate isolated high-resolution image at consistent scale, camera, material, and lighting.
- Export transparent PNG and WebP files for production use.
- Treat AI-generated contact sheets and exploded-parts boards as reference images only.
- Build continuous articulated motion manually in Blender or Rive from an approved master; a flattened generated image is not a rig.
- Recreate interface marks, warning symbols, measurement lines, and ChairBot face graphics as native SVG layers.
