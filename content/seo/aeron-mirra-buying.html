Aeron or Mirra 2: which should you shortlist?

Our research-based starting point is to try Aeron if you want to compare its A, B and C sizes. Try Mirra 2 if its choice of back constructions or optional FlexFront seat-depth adjustment interests you. This is a configuration comparison, not a hands-on comfort test. We have not established a universal winner.

Fit checks that matter more than a height cutoff

We do not recommend Mirra 2 automatically to anyone above a particular height, or impose a blanket height ceiling on Aeron. Compare the exact configuration's dimensions with your seated measurements and desk. During a trial, check foot support, clearance behind your knees, arm position and contact with the seat edge. A specification sheet cannot establish your comfort or predict pain relief.

For Mirra 2, confirm whether the quoted chair includes FlexFront and which back type it has. For Aeron, confirm the size and generation before comparing measurements. Our advice is to trial the same configuration you plan to buy, not merely another chair carrying the same model name.
