Leap V2 or Karman: where to start

Our research-based recommendation is to shortlist Leap if you prefer upholstered seating and manual back-resistance adjustment. Shortlist Karman if you want to try a suspension seat with integrated cushioning and a weight-activated mechanism. Neither design guarantees better comfort. We have not hands-on tested these chairs, and we do not select a winner using unverified review averages.

Weight and fit

Steelcase publishes a Karman weight of 29 lb (approximately 13.2 kg), rather than 5 kg. Confirm the weight of the selected variant if moving it matters. We have not established a like-for-like weight ratio between these listings.

Compare the seller's exact seat dimensions and adjustment ranges with your desk and body measurements. Do not infer a universal height range or comfort advantage from one seat-depth number. During a trial, check thigh clearance, arm position and recline resistance.

Before choosing a retailer

Compare quotes for the same condition and configuration. Include delivery, tax, return freight and any restocking charge. Ask who provides warranty service in your country, especially for used, refurbished or imported chairs.
