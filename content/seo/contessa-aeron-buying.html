Contessa II or Aeron: which should you shortlist?

Shortlist Contessa II if a manufacturer-listed headrest option or upholstered seat is important. Shortlist Aeron if you want to compare three chair sizes with a suspension seat and back. Neither feature list establishes which will feel better for you. This is a research-based comparison, not a hands-on test.

Fit checks before checkout

Our recommendation is to check the exact chair's seat height, seat depth, arm clearance and return conditions against your desk and body measurements. We do not assign either model a universal user-height range or infer comfort from its weight. Try the configuration you intend to order when practical.

Compare the total purchase terms

Ask each seller for an itemized quote covering the chair configuration, delivery, taxes and any assembly charge. Confirm who handles returns, return freight and warranty service in your country. We have not verified live prices, stock, delivery eligibility or seller-specific coverage, so this comparison does not declare a price or warranty winner.
