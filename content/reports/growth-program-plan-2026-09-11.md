# US traffic and affiliate growth program

## Objective and limits
Improve qualified overseas traffic and confirmed affiliate commissions, with the US as the first market. Keep existing regional routing. No offline sales objective, fabricated tests, paid ranking links, copied news expansion or mass publication of near-duplicate pages.
This is a 12-week operating plan, not a promise to complete 12 weeks of observation today or a ranking/revenue guarantee. No background monitoring is scheduled.

## Execution order
1. Inventory published database routes and the live sitemap. Classify keep, improve, consolidation review and buying-path review using explicit flags. Store complete URL-level output. Flags are screening, not deletion decisions or Google penalties.
2. Improve the shared buying-guide surface: remove runtime freshness claims, unsupported testing/winner labels and live-price implications; make a shortlist navigable; keep tracked regional purchase links and place disclosure near them.
3. Improve the existing online buying hub: use the common catalog resolver, label availability honestly, show product imagery and connect directly to Amazon while retaining product-detail links. Do not create new competing URLs.
4. Verify scoped logic, regressions, production build, deployment and mobile/desktop rendering. Intercept analytics and retailer traffic during browser tests.
5. Review real GSC/GA4 observations; do not equate generated browser events with received analytics, affiliate clicks with orders, or reported cities with human buyers.

## Weekly sequence
| Period | Work | Acceptance gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Week 1 | Inventory, common buying templates, measurement baseline | Reproducible URL inventory; build and live regression tests |
| Weeks 2-4 | Prioritized US intent clusters and overlapping content review | Retained URL justified by relevance/search data; contextual internal links; no automatic deletion |
| Weeks 2-4 | Original chair measurements/photos pilot | Real collected evidence, methods and source dates; no invented measurements |
| Weeks 5-8 | Expand useful comparisons and repurpose original visual evidence | Source ownership confirmed; distribution follows platform rules |
| Weeks 9-12 | Compare cohorts and affiliate reports; reallocate effort | Confirmed commissions net of returns where available; distinguish reporting periods and uncertainty |

## Work lanes and dependencies
- Engineering: shared presentation and link resolution, accessible mobile comparison, source/price labeling and automated audits.
- Editorial: resolve duplicate intent, verify claims and retain useful content. Redirect only relevant replaced pages after evidence and backup.
- Evidence: collect actual dimensions and original media. Physical measurements require access to real chairs; code cannot manufacture them.
- Measurement: GSC impressions/clicks, GA4 country/source/page engagement and affiliate_click; Amazon ordered/shipped items and commissions from actual reports. Attribution across systems is not assumed to be user-level.
- No broad paid acquisition until revenue per qualified visit is supported by real data.

## Success metrics
Primary: confirmed affiliate commissions. Leading indicators: qualified US landing sessions, buying-link click rate on supported page groups, country/retailer click mix. Search visibility is a diagnostic, not a substitute for revenue.
Use before/after matched periods and annotate deployments. Small samples are directional, not causal proof. Indexing is not guaranteed by a sitemap or structured data.
